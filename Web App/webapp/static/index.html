The assistant answers three questions about one chest X-ray and writes them up as a short report:

  • Is there pneumonia? Two DenseNet121 classifiers decide, with a calibrated probability.
  • Where? A YOLO detector marks the region; the result names the lung and zone.
  • What does it mean? A fine-tuned GPT-Neo writes the report and answers your questions, using only these findings.

Trained and tested on the RSNA Pneumonia Detection dataset (test set: accuracy 0.954, sensitivity 0.907, specificity 0.986). Other lung abnormalities are often flagged as well; the model does not tell them apart from pneumonia.
